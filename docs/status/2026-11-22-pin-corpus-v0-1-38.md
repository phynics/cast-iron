# Corpus pinned to v0.1.38, 2026-11-22

`sources/corpus.toml` now pins `phynics/cast-iron-data` tag `v0.1.38`. The annotated tag object is
`7c87674fdb2ef17507beac1869e2d1d525c7498c`; it peels to commit
`5f4f84cfd394e6f8d205da021da334812759dc4b`. The tree hash is
`a4f161d1de37e136e319f919db6f222466c37cd0065e49e06d0c3c58764031f5` (`cargo xtask corpus-tree-hash` on the
extracted codeload archive). No entry is added, removed or moved, and no `id` or alias changes; 7,601 entry
files change in body or fields. The pin switches three closed-vocabulary checks from warning to error (see
below), and no other compiler change is part of it.

- **The Linnorm table of the Draconic bloodline** (data #165, data PR #172, refs
  [#594](https://github.com/phynics/cast-iron/issues/594)). *Ultimate Magic* p. 71 prints no table. The
  corpus table is compiled from the breath weapons of the linnorm monsters, labelled with a `Compiled table`
  note, and three rows are corrected. The Gare and Sea rows could not be checked against print.
- **Feat types are a closed list** (data #169, data PR #174). A book abbreviation (`BoA`, `UW`), a title
  qualifier (`Taldor Variant`) and a race (`Dhampir`) leave `feat_types`. A feat that print gives no type
  stays untyped in the corpus, and the compiler gives it the facet `General`
  ([decision](../decisions/2026-11-22-filter-fields-have-closed-vocabularies.md)). Creature size is closed
  too.
- **Affliction types and item slots** (data #171, data PRs #177 and #185). Each affliction carries one type
  tag. Variant slot spellings go to the printed slot, and the owner decisions of 2026-11-22 are applied
  ([decision](../decisions/2026-11-22-affliction-types-and-item-slots-are-closed-vocabularies.md)). 9 `slot`
  values in 9 entries stay open by owner decision.
- **Title echoes are dropped** (data #168, data PRs #178 to #182). The bold name paragraph of creatures, the
  bold title paragraph of traps and templates, the `## Name (Type)` heading of feats, the `Type` row and
  `Benefits` heading of PSRD mythic feats (now the Pf Data form), and the `Type:` line of traits that
  repeats the category no longer appear in the body: the header shows the title and the facts line shows
  the type ([#574](https://github.com/phynics/cast-iron/issues/574)).
