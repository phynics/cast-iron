# Corpus pinned to v0.1.46, 2026-11-24

`sources/corpus.toml` now pins `phynics/cast-iron-data` tag `v0.1.46`, commit
`d3381782437a217b2caa87db40b93dd41c409170`. The tree hash is
`6565c1fb070ac6ab86e542efb67abc8482a0db141df385a4f1d6edfe50cef201` (computed with

entries and 443 publications. The data CI on the tag was green.

  class mirrors, race hubs and single entries lose books they do not print. 63 `expanded_in` credits exist

  Spymaster's Handbook p. 22. Moon Hag loses Blood of the Coven and its copied coven section. Page counts of many
  books drop. Books changed: Advanced Player's Guide, Monster Codex, Ultimate Equipment.
