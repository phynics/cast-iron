# Corpus pinned to v0.1.42, 2026-11-24

`sources/corpus.toml` now pins `phynics/cast-iron-data` tag `v0.1.42`. The annotated tag object is
`8ef473af95da2a5c8a3eb19b977f064dfab57088`; it peels to commit
`8c89e35ed332284412cdd19a61e18d27538b8071`. The tree hash is
`8ab3d59ff34efd978135ef8ab41934ce93be22ba8229e3426e45eeec598eec32` (`cargo xtask corpus-tree-hash` on the
extracted codeload archive). The tag carries data PRs #268 to #273. 329 entry files change, seven entry files are
deleted and retired to aliases, and no entry is added. No `id` is edited and no compiler change is part of the
pin. Entries: 31,742 to 31,735.

- **Seven retired entries.** Each one duplicated a canonical entry and now resolves through `aliases.toml`
  (basis `shared`): the carried Rabies and Ghoul Fever (to `ref/disease/rabies` and `ref/disease/ghoul-fever`),
  the carried Shared Wrath and Tireless Pursuers (to `ref/spell/shared-wrath` and `ref/spell/tireless-pursuers`),
  the `eq-reagent` Dragon's Gut and Odor Stalk (to the `eq-misc` entries of the same slug), and the Ultimate
  Equipment armor-and-shields Madu (to the Madu of the weapon descriptions). Their book nodes leave the books
  of Advanced Player's Guide, Bestiary and Ultimate Equipment, and `exclusions.toml` records each as a
  `[[rejected]]` duplicate.
  By kind: affliction 468 to 466, item 8,100 to 8,097, spell 3,088 to 3,086; the other kinds are unchanged.
- **Printed pages.** 355 `[[sources]]` rows gain a printed page. Fourteen Faiths and Philosophies traits cite
  `page = "inside cover"`; the corpus reader already treats that as a clean page with no leading number
  (`lead_page` in `crates/corpus/src/books.rs`).
- **Hand stat-block work.** Composite bows, the emerald wedge, net-rigging (two titled trap blocks, `CR 1/2`),
  the duergar tyrant, brambleblight and the lavender ioun pair (now split) change. Ghoul Fever's text loses
  "(advanced ghoul)". The Serpentfolk Venom Fortitude DC is 15 (was 25).
