# Corpus pinned to v0.1.43, 2026-11-24

`sources/corpus.toml` now pins `phynics/cast-iron-data` tag `v0.1.43`. The annotated tag object is
`afa880baf9a8c5223b29bf18fa74ba444e7e3006`; it peels to commit `8f142f076fb0c75e04fb024f89a5654e7ce3c0cb`.
The tree hash is `4ffaf121e917818b6fe3d0238ddb363f2ac197539c3e6f7db9ae5cd572248116`
(`cargo xtask corpus-tree-hash` on the extracted codeload archive). The tag carries data PRs #274, #275, #278,
#279, #280, #288 and #290. 297 entry files change, two entry files are retired to aliases, and no entry is
added. No `id` is edited. Entries: 31,735 to 31,733. This pin is PR 3 of
[#618](https://github.com/phynics/cast-iron/issues/618) and completes its code; the schema upgrade check comes
with the release.

- **Haunts.** The 45 `entries/haunt/` files now have `kind = "haunt"`. Their ids and paths are unchanged. By
  kind: haunt 0 to 45, rule 6,026 to 5,981; the other kinds are unchanged except trait (below). Horror
  Adventures holds 26 of them (Horror Realms 6, Occult Adventures 5, Ultimate Wilderness 3, Pathfinder #144
  2, Book of the Damned, Construct Handbook and GameMastery Guide 1 each).
- **Two retired entries.** The carried Advanced Player's Guide traits `divine-courtesan` and
  `favored-son-daughter` duplicated `ref/trait/calistrian-courtesan` and `ref/trait/favored-son-or-daughter`
  (titles `Calistrian Courtesan` and `Favored Son/Daughter`). Each resolves through `aliases.toml` (basis
  `shared`); trait 1,937 to 1,935. Both ids are in `sources/published-ids.txt` and in the new `aliases.json`.
- **Copy that follows print.** 35 disease Effect lines (for example Rabies and Ghoul Fever), the Spellscorch
  type (`injury`), the pit fiend poison (`1/round for 10 rounds`); Shared Wrath, Tireless Pursuers and Madu
  lose some lines. Five NPCs with mounts are now two stat sets each, the mounts with `Challenge rating: -`.
- **Printed pages.** About 200 more citation pages; some use `page = "inside cover"`, which the Sources line
  now prints as `, inside cover` ([#667](https://github.com/phynics/cast-iron/pull/667)).
