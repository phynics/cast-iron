# Corpus pinned to v0.1.53, 2026-11-25

`sources/corpus.toml` now pins `phynics/cast-iron-data` tag `v0.1.53`, commit
`46fd82ec7eee2ddf4973443c0654a123a68ab5e2`. The tree hash is
`b6fab5681072fa55bd1aa7c7bbba5ca78e2092f7d6f652dfa24a2efd5310bbf5` (computed with
`cargo xtask corpus-tree-hash` on the extracted codeload archive; `cargo xtask fetch-sources` verified it).
The previous pin was v0.1.51. This pin skips v0.1.52, whose changes v0.1.53 contains.

Between v0.1.51 and v0.1.53, 116 entry files changed. None was added, deleted or moved and no id changed; the
library still has 31,672 entries and 443 books. The data CI on the tag was green. The changes come from four
data PRs:

- **#320** ([#461](https://github.com/phynics/cast-iron/issues/461)) adds `membership-exceptions.toml` at the
  corpus root. It has one row: Gamemastery in the GameMastery Guide, until the home-book question
  (phynics/cast-iron-data#277) is ruled. App PR [#715](https://github.com/phynics/cast-iron/pull/715) reads it.
- **#317** ([#540](https://github.com/phynics/cast-iron/issues/540)) converts 18 archetypes that waited for the
  reader of [#710](https://github.com/phynics/cast-iron/pull/710) to printed prose: 12 oracle archetypes, Deep
  Bomber, Strangler, Verminous Hunter, Eldritch Scion, Spell Warrior and Naturalist.
- **#319** (#540) converts Daring Infiltrator (Advanced Class Guide p. 124) and Ironskin Monk (Advanced Race Guide
  pp. 122-123), which need the reader of [#714](https://github.com/phynics/cast-iron/pull/714). Every archetype of
  the Advanced Class Guide and the Advanced Race Guide is now printed prose.
- **#318** reads in print what earlier lacked a source: 349 citation pages, 10 Effect lines (the 9 disease
  entries and Void Death, Pathfinder #14 p. 81, now 1d3 Dex and 1d3 Con), the Tetrodotoxin title (the id keeps its old spelling),
  Brambleblight split into two stat sets (anchors `stat-animated-bramble` and `stat-brambleblight`), Azure
  Briolette's price and slot, and the folded stat rows of nine Adventure Path creatures. Bubonic Plague of the
  daughter of Urgathoa leaves Pathfinder #8; Sea Urchin Venom moves from Pathfinder #56 to Pathfinder #37 p. 85.
