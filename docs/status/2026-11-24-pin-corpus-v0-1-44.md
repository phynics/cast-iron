# Corpus pinned to v0.1.44, 2026-11-24

`sources/corpus.toml` now pins `phynics/cast-iron-data` tag `v0.1.44`, commit
`eea332670d1e6cca8d207e70291d74b18adf090e`. The tree hash is
`b8d059ef2a163a7515026568fda5848eecb87e3e53fb127b1208d96bfd18bcd2` (the tree-hash rule of `sources/corpus.toml`
on the extracted codeload archive; `cargo xtask fetch-sources` verified it). The tag carries data PRs #286, #293,
#295, #291, #294, #298, #297 and #299. 120 entry files change, 61 entry files are retired to aliases, and no
entry is added. No `id` is edited. Entries: 31,733 to 31,672 (spell 3,086 to 3,029, affliction 466 to 462).

- **Retired: 57 carried spells.** Carried Pathfinder Reference Document spells with inverted titles
  (`Restoration, Greater`, `Planar Ally, Lesser`, `Reduce Person, Mass`, `Scale Spikes, Greater`, `Discharge,
  Greater` and the like) duplicated their Pathfinder Data twins (`Greater Restoration`, `Lesser Planar Ally`,
  `Mass Reduce Person` ...). Each retired id resolves through `aliases.toml` to its canonical `ref/spell/<slug>`
  twin. The carried sources are Core Rulebook (39), Advanced Player's Guide (14), Technology Guide (3) and
  Monster Codex (1).
- **Retired: 4 carried madnesses.** The GameMastery Guide entries `mania-phobia`, `psychosis`, `paranoia` and
  `multiple-personality-disorder` resolve to `ref/madness/mania`, `ref/madness/moral-insanity`,
  `ref/madness/paranoid` and `ref/madness/dissociated-identity`. The surviving madnesses gain the
  GameMastery Guide text and a GameMastery Guide source row (Mania, p. 250, with Horror Adventures p. 184).
  All 61 retired ids are in `sources/published-ids.txt`; the published-id guard passed and each is in the new
  `aliases.json`.
- **16 titles follow print.** Environment Weapon, Expose Weakness, Sucker-Punch, Fight Response, Accursed,
  Expert Cipher, Mischievous Smite, Complex Countermeasure, Dragon's Breath Focus, Mother's Eyes, Iconoclastic
  Strike, Seamless Shapechanger, Life Review, Improvised Weapon Affinity, Dirty Strike and Asmodean Demon
  Hunter. Ids and paths are unchanged.
- **Book memberships.** The standard racial traits of the core races list only the books that print them, and
  each Figurines of Wondrous Power entry lists only the variant its book prints. The pages and counts of 30
  books drop: Advanced Player's Guide 1,567 to 1,495, Advanced Class Guide 915 to 851, Horror Adventures 628 to
  567, Core Rulebook 2,842 to 2,790, Ultimate Combat 1,071 to 1,049 (22 Figurines placements) and so on; Game
  Mastery Guide loses 4, Technology Guide 3, Monster Codex 1. The book files of Advanced Player's Guide, Core
  Rulebook, GameMastery Guide, Monster Codex, Technology Guide, Ultimate Campaign and Ultimate Combat change.
  (The counts are the entry placements of the compiled `books.json`, old library against new.)
- **Print fixes.** Five spell entries follow print.
