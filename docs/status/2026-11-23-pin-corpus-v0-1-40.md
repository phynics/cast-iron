# Corpus pinned to v0.1.40, 2026-11-23

`sources/corpus.toml` now pins `phynics/cast-iron-data` tag `v0.1.40`. The annotated tag object is
`6e083250f136a1117dcb11f74043c8b3db2aaf0b`; it peels to commit
`ca316adfc791b7e090e76184a780496f60dcd861`. The tree hash is
`fca4ea8352ef7620a330b4cddb97dceec21f544f1f1386aba02ccfa81a86050a` (`cargo xtask corpus-tree-hash` on the
extracted codeload archive). The tag carries data PRs #206 to #229. 15,328 entry files change; no entry file
is added, removed or moved, no `id` is edited, and `aliases.toml`, `exclusions.toml`, `legal.toml`,
`publications.toml` and the curated `books/` files are byte-identical to v0.1.39. No compiler change is part
of the pin.

- **Citation rows** (data issue [#170](https://github.com/phynics/cast-iron-data/issues/170)). Traits, racial
  traits, class options, afflictions, mythic feats, traps and skills get `[[sources]]` rows with their pages,
  and the `**Sources:**` lines they replace leave the bodies. The corpus goes from 30,823 to 35,770
  `[[sources]]` rows. An entry page now prints the page in its Sources line, for example `Sources: Mythic
  Adventures p. 71` on Power Attack, Mythic.
- **Stat blocks** (data issue [#166](https://github.com/phynics/cast-iron-data/issues/166)). 2,828 monsters,
  440 NPCs and 7,550 items state their statistics in `::: statblock` blocks under `###` headings (`Defense`,
  `Offense`, `Statistics`, `Ecology`, `Construction`) in place of one bold-label paragraph per value. Files
  with a stat block go from 4,171 to 14,989.
- **Weapon group anchors.** The 17 headings of `ref/ability/weapon-groups` carry `{#sec-…}` anchors, and the
  `Weapon Group` row of a weapon links to its section.
- **Print fixes** to entry text.
