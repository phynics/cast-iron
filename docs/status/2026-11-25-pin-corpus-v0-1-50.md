# Corpus pinned to v0.1.50, 2026-11-25

`sources/corpus.toml` now pins `phynics/cast-iron-data` tag `v0.1.50`, commit
`8f1efbd68b883f7eaf81e4f3561c296bb2986e4b`. The tree hash is
`2c8307edf27d062bbabe4d0af04748d053875fbb6f06c78c3901be28a2667111` (computed with
`cargo xtask corpus-tree-hash` on the extracted codeload archive; `cargo xtask fetch-sources` verified it).

The tag holds v0.1.48 ([#540](https://github.com/phynics/cast-iron/issues/540) batch 1), v0.1.49 (batch 2) and
the v0.1.50 fix, so this pin takes all three. Since v0.1.47 the corpus changed 285 archetype files (126 in v0.1.48,
158 in v0.1.49, one more in v0.1.50: Beast Master of the Advanced Player's Guide; the other four files of v0.1.50 are
among the 126) and added or deleted none; the library still has 31,672 entries and 443 books. The data CI on the tag
was green.

- **v0.1.48 (batch 1):** 126 Ultimate Magic and Ultimate Combat archetypes become headed printed prose with
  `features` (heads with `Level N`, search aliases) and `trades[].by`. Copy follows print: Sound Striker, the
  Evangelist's spontaneous-casting table, Bladebound's Black Blade subsection and its unlisted Magus Arcana
  section, the Qinggong Monk's ki power tables per level, the Brawler's Expanded Weapon Groups section. About 20
  archetypes use the printed trade names (Divine Hunter `Aura of faith`, Reanimator and Psychonaut `Increase to
  bomb damage at this level`, Musket Master `Gun training 1, 2, 3, and 4`). Geisha, Infiltrator, Dervish Dancer
  and Reanimator change an op from alters to replaces. The woodland stride trade is dropped from Dragon, Saurian
  and Shark Shaman.
- **v0.1.49 (batch 2):** 158 Advanced Class Guide (97 of 104) and Advanced Race Guide (61 of 68) archetypes become
  headed printed prose with `features` and `trades[].by`. Option lists nest as unlisted `###` sections (Swordmaster
  trances, Spell Specialist exploits, Naga Aspirant forms and others). About 30 stored trade names follow print
  (`Bonus feat gained at 4th level`, `Mercy gained at 3rd level`). Blood God Disciple's summon monster trades are
  one trade, and Buccaneer's unsupported `Gun training 3` trade is dropped. `scripts/errata.tsv` gains Advanced
  Class Guide errata rows (Ecclesitheurge's Blessing of the Faithful and Bonded Holy Symbol, Bolt Ace's Crossbow
  Maven, Sniper's Deadly Sniper, Divine Tracker's opening).
- **v0.1.50:** five `features` rows are removed: `armor-proficiency` from Beast Rider, Emissary and Gun Tank, and
  `class-skills` from Beast Master and Trapper. The headings stay as plain headings, so those five archetypes lose
  the feature head and the search alias for Armor Proficiency or Class Skills.
