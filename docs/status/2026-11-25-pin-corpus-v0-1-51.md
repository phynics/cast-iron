# Corpus pinned to v0.1.51, 2026-11-25

`689deb0e0a290ea3c2eb952f636e2f218b71046f`. The tree hash is
`1e8b1036d08abc794efbee8e43cadccaf1160f5b5537e16547eb5dc76cf874f6` (computed with

player's guides ([#461](https://github.com/phynics/cast-iron/issues/461), slice 12). The corpus changed 64 entry

green. The warning for memberships without a page citation fell from 43 to 1 (Gamemastery, GameMastery Guide).
