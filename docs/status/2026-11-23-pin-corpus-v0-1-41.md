# Corpus pinned to v0.1.41, 2026-11-23

`sources/corpus.toml` now pins `phynics/cast-iron-data` tag `v0.1.41`. The annotated tag object is
`c5f134fb0510e0f02f27d7b2d8dbb2ae4a586b74`; it peels to commit
`8545bf12f1025d896aee7e279031be9c8923bf7a`. The tree hash is
`cc40ef525811e151ccbb5ec7fcb6e8f3b4abb26396d8805bc82825885f149747` (`cargo xtask corpus-tree-hash` on the
extracted codeload archive). The tag carries data PRs #230 to #266. 741 entry files change; no entry file is
added, removed or moved and no `id` is edited. No compiler change is part of the pin. This pin closes
[phynics/cast-iron-data#171](https://github.com/phynics/cast-iron-data/issues/171).

- **Titles.** Four traits (Demonblight Resistance, Sodden Child, Wasteland Vagrant, Emberkin Impostor) and eight
  rules take their printed titles: the seven planes `Heaven`, `Nirvana`, `Elysium`, `Axis`, `The Maelstrom`,
  `Hell` and `Abaddon` (they had a code-like title such as `Heaven_pa`), and `Races (Pursuits)`. The seven
  planes lose a bold first paragraph that repeated the title.
- **Stat blocks** (data issue [#166](https://github.com/phynics/cast-iron-data/issues/166)). More items and
  monsters state their statistics in stat blocks: tech, miscellaneous equipment, ioun stones, wondrous items,
  spellbooks, weapons, artifacts and siege engines. New row labels are `Arcane spell failure`, `Category` on its
  own row, and `Price` and `Weight` as separate rows. Intelligent items get a block and a paragraph.
- **Real stat sets.** Twelve creatures now have two or more `##` sets, which the Stat sets list shows: Air,
  Aether, Earth, Fire, Ice, Lightning, Magma, Mud and Water Elemental (six sets each), Waldgeist, Benaioh and the
  goblin vulture pilot (two each).
- **Sources.** Some raw `**Sources:**` lines become `[[sources]]` rows. The ioun stone rows read Seekers of
  Secrets pp. 44 to 50, and the elemental citations are page ranges.
- **Slots and prerequisites.** Garden of Sulesh and two rods have the slot `none`. Catch Off-Guard (Teitr) gains
  `prerequisites`.
