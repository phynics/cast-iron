# Publication filter keeps a heading for every tier, 2026-11-06

Refs [#132](https://github.com/phynics/cast-iron/issues/132), a follow-up to #101 and to "Publication filter groups by tier" above. On a broad query such as `fire`, 27 Rulebooks match and the twelve-chip budget went entirely to them, so no other tier showed and only "of 319" hinted that they existed. The owner chose option C: keep the budget and the Rulebooks-first order, and give every tier with results a heading.

- **Every non-Rulebook tier is a native `<details>`.** Rulebooks stay a plain group. A tier with results always renders its heading with the number of publications with results that it holds ("Player Companions (87)"), even when none of its chips fit the budget. A tier with no result and no selection stays absent. One disclosure for all four lower tiers replaces the special case for Adventures, so every tier behaves the same way. The alternative, a "Show all" button per tier, would add buttons where a heading already exists, and a native disclosure keeps keyboard and screen-reader behaviour free.
- **Opening a tier lists all of its publications.** The reader's answer is stored per tier in `group_choice`, as before, and `publication_page` takes it as input. A tier the reader has opened (and every tier under "Show all" or the name box) lists every publication with results; those chips spend no budget. A tier that is not opened lists only its budget chips, which for a tier the budget missed is none. The chips stay out of the DOM until then, so the first page stays small (an Adventures group is over a hundred chips).
- **The first view still follows the budget, and the rule is about the budget.** The twelve slots are spent in `Tier::ALL` order, so a Rulebook with results is never hidden while an unselected lower-tier publication shows. A reader who opens a lower tier asks for it explicitly, so its chips can appear while Rulebooks 13 and later still wait behind "Show all". Selected publications keep spending the budget first, unless their tier is already listing everything.
- **A collapsed-by-default tier takes no budget.** Adventures start closed, so chips given to it would sit in a closed disclosure and would have to be counted as hidden. It is skipped when the budget is spent, and the leftover goes to the next tier. Other can therefore fill slots that Adventures would have taken. Adventures stay closed by default and open under a selection, the name box, "Show all", or the reader.
- **Adventures opens alone, and a forced close is not a choice.** When Adventures is the only tier with results, it opens by default and spends up to `PUBLICATION_TOP` chips, because a page of "Showing 0 of N" and one closed heading looks empty; a reader's explicit close still wins. A close click on a tier that a selection or a non-empty name box forces open is answered by reopening it and records nothing, so clearing the box restores the answer the reader gave before (#141, #142).
- **A selection forces its tier open, and the name box opens every tier.** These override a stored "closed" answer, as before. A tier opened only by a selection, for example from a `source=` link, lists the selected chip and its budget share, not the whole tier; its heading reads "Adventures (1 of 135)", and opening and closing it, or "Show all", lists the rest.
- **The summary line counts only chips a reader can see.** "Showing X of Y publications with results" counts listed publications in open groups; chips in a closed group, or a group the reader closed, are not counted. #124 counted chips in a closed Adventures group as shown; that is dropped because it made the line claim chips behind a closed heading. Y is every publication with results, so X plus "Show all (N more)" is Y, and the heading counts add up to Y. The name-box wording and the no-"1 of 0" guarantee of #109 are unchanged. "Showing all Y" appears only when nothing is left unseen; if the reader has closed a tier under "Show all", the line falls back to "X of Y".
- **An open heading that lists only some of its tier says so.** "Rulebooks (12 of 27)" and "Player Companions (7 of 87)" count the chips in the list; a closed heading and a fully listed one show the plain count. The Rulebooks heading carries a count for the same reason.
- **"Show all" and the headings do not conflict.** "Show all" lists every tier and opens it by default, including Adventures, because it is an explicit request for everything and a closed group would break "Showing all". A tier the reader closed stays closed, and the line says so. The button's "N more" is everything not currently visible, headings included.
- **Unchanged from #109 and #124.** Zero-count publications are hidden; selected publications are pinned; the name box filters by title and shows every match; counts follow the query and the other filters; the keyed lists keep focus order and disposal as before. Groups are `role="group"` with `aria-labelledby`, headings are plain text rather than pills, and each summary is at least 44 px tall.
