# Archetype list and compatibility filter on class pages, 2026-11-22

Refs [#170](https://github.com/phynics/cast-iron/issues/170), part D. It builds the compatibility matcher that `docs/corpus-format.md` ("Two different comparisons") left open, on the format 2 trades of #450 and the migration of #452. The owner decided four points on 2026-11-22 after the review of #599 (comment on #170); they are in the bullets below.

- **The list replaces the printed blurbs and the matrix.** A class page printed each archetype as a bold name, `Class:`, `Modifies or Replaces:` and `Description:`, then a matrix of X marks (`Archetypes by Feature`, 19 columns for the Wizard). Now the Archetypes section holds one row per archetype of the active books: the name (a link to its own page), the book, and what it replaces (`Replaces …`, then `Alters …` when it alters features). The matrix and the blurbs leave the class page, because the rows and the filter carry the same facts from typed data and the archetype's own page has its text. The introduction of the section stays. The list sits right under the heading, ahead of the introduction, so the Archetypes stop of the switcher lands on it. The change is a pure transform of the typed body (`archetypes::fold_body`), not a change to the corpus: a blurb is a bold name followed by a `Class:` row, the matrix is the section titled `Archetypes by Feature`. A body whose Archetypes heading the page cannot find keeps its blurbs, its matrix and its old panel, so a class never loses its archetypes; a class without a switcher still gets the list, which opens at the heading itself.
- **Compatibility comes from the recorded trades only (owner, 2026-11-22).** The matrix is not shown on the page, and no other source feeds the filter: not the printed matrix, not the text of an archetype. While a choice is active the page says so in a hint under the count line. Where the trades do not place a feature the filter says that it cannot check, and does not guess. The gaps in the trades data are tracked in [phynics/cast-iron-data#173](https://github.com/phynics/cast-iron-data/issues/173). DESIGN.md says the same.
- **Archetypes that are not in the library lose their blurbs too (owner).** Jungle Lord, Sword-Devil, Warlord, Treesinger, Sorrowblade, Elegist, Vellemancer and Grenadier are printed in a class body but have no entry in the library. The list shows archetypes of the library only, so their blurbs leave the class page with the others. They stay in the coverage report (`exclusions.toml` of the corpus, rule 6); nothing is dropped silently.
- **Two archetypes conflict when a trade of one overlaps a trade of the other.** Overlap is the corpus format's compatibility matcher, per level and per power (owner): the same feature anchor, a shared level (a trade without `levels` covers every level, so a whole feature overlaps any of its levels), a shared named power (a trade without a `power` covers every power; two powers must be equal, ignoring case), and the same class when both name one. Different levels of one feature, or different named powers, do not conflict. **Replacements and alterations both count.** Pathfinder's archetype rule bars two archetypes that change the same feature, and the printed matrix is headed `Modifies or Replaces`; a filter that let two archetypes alter the same feature would offer an illegal pair. The issue's wording ("replace") is the common case, and the row's `Replaces` line stays as the printed replacements.
- **Features to keep.** An archetype touches a feature when one of its resolved trades names the feature's heading anchor, whatever the levels or power: keeping a feature whole is lost by a change to any part of it. The `Keep` menu lists only the features that at least one archetype touches, in the order of the class's own headings (the explicit anchors of #252), labelled by the heading without its ability tag. A feature with no heading is named from its anchor (`bonus-feats` is `Bonus feats`), never by the printed name of one trade, which can name one level (`5th-level bonus feat`).
- **Unresolved is never compatible.** A trade without a `feature`, or an archetype with no trades row, proves nothing. Such an archetype (in the pinned corpus v0.1.37: 299 of 5,890 trades are unresolved, 92 of 1,245 archetypes with a replacement have one, and 16 archetypes have no trades row) is listed apart under `Not checked` while a choice is active, so the reader neither loses it nor reads it as a fit. The hint under the group says what the data lacks: for an archetype with no trades row, that the data does not say which class features it changes; for one with trades, that some trade names no class feature.
- **A chosen archetype that cannot be checked blocks the list.** When the `Combine with` archetype has an unresolved trade, has no trades row, or is an id that is not among the rows (a shared link to an archetype of another book or class), it could clash with any of the others. The list then shows nothing as compatible, every other row stands under `Not checked`, and the hint names the reason. The list never compares only the resolved part of the chosen archetype. Data counts: 5,890 trades (4,755 replace, 1,135 alter), 5,591 resolved (95%), 1,019 with `levels`, 538 with a `power`, none with a `class` (no archetype has more than one class).
- **The choices are the route's (owner).** `?keep=<anchor>` (once per feature, in the order chosen) and `?with=<archetype id>` ride beside `a`, `book` and `s` on the entry address, for example `#/entry/ref/class/wizard?s=archetypes&keep=arcane-bond&with=ref%2Farc-wizard%2Farcane-physician`. A choice is a history entry, so Back undoes it and a shared link opens filtered; an address without them parses as before. They follow `section`: the page is keyed on the entry, anchor and book and is not rebuilt for a choice, and the frame leaves scroll and focus alone when only the choices change. A stop of the section switcher keeps the choices. DESIGN.md's rule that filter state is route state holds here as on the list pages. The two menus are the shared multi-select and single-choice panels of `views/filter_menu.rs`; the multi-select panel gained the find field above ten options that the single-choice one has, because a class can have 26 touched features.
- **The old panel goes.** The class page's `Archetypes` disclosure (a list grouped by book) is not drawn when the list is in the page; the list shows the book on each row. The `Show 1 archetypes` plural was already fixed in the panel; the count line uses the same `pluralize`. The count leaves the chosen archetype out of its total.
