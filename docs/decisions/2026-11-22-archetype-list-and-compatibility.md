# Archetype list and compatibility filter on class pages, 2026-11-22

Refs [#170](https://github.com/phynics/cast-iron/issues/170), part D. It builds the compatibility matcher that `docs/corpus-format.md` ("Two different comparisons") left open, on the format 2 trades of #450 and the migration of #452.

- **The list replaces the printed blurbs and the matrix.** A class page printed each archetype as a bold name, `Class:`, `Modifies or Replaces:` and `Description:`, then a matrix of X marks (`Archetypes by Feature`, 19 columns for the Wizard). Now the Archetypes section holds one row per archetype of the active books: the name (a link to its own page), the book, and what it replaces (`Replaces …`, then `Alters …` when it alters features). The matrix and the blurbs leave the class page, because the rows and the filter carry the same facts from typed data and the archetype's own page has its text. The introduction of the section stays. The list sits right under the heading, ahead of the introduction, so the Archetypes stop of the switcher lands on it. The change is a pure transform of the typed body (`archetypes::fold_body`), not a change to the corpus: a blurb is a bold name followed by a `Class:` row, the matrix is the section titled `Archetypes by Feature`, and a body without a top-level `Archetypes` section keeps its text and the old panel.
- **Two archetypes conflict when a trade of one overlaps a trade of the other.** Overlap is the corpus format's compatibility matcher: the same feature anchor, a shared level (a trade without `levels` covers every level, so a whole feature overlaps any of its levels) and a shared named power (a trade without a `power` covers every power; two powers must be equal), and the same class when both name one. **Replacements and alterations both count.** Pathfinder's archetype rule bars two archetypes that change the same feature, and the printed matrix is headed `Modifies or Replaces`; a filter that let two archetypes alter the same feature would offer an illegal pair. The issue's wording ("replace") is the common case, and the row's `Replaces` line stays as the printed replacements.
- **Features to keep.** An archetype touches a feature when one of its resolved trades names the feature's heading anchor, whatever the levels or power: keeping a feature whole is lost by a change to any part of it. The `Keep` menu lists only the features that at least one archetype touches, in the order of the class's own headings (the explicit anchors of #252), labelled by the heading without its ability tag; a feature with no heading is named by the printed name of its first trade.
- **Unresolved is never compatible.** A trade without a `feature`, or an archetype with no trades row, proves nothing. Such an archetype (in the pinned corpus v0.1.37: 299 of 5,890 trades are unresolved, 92 of 1,245 archetypes with a replacement have one, and 16 archetypes have no trades row) is listed apart under `Not checked` while a choice is active, so the reader neither loses it nor reads it as a fit. If the chosen archetype of `Combine with` has an unresolved trade, the list compares its resolved trades and says so in a line. Data counts: 5,890 trades (4,755 replace, 1,135 alter), 5,591 resolved (95%), 1,019 with `levels`, 538 with a `power`, none with a `class` (no archetype has more than one class).
- **The state is the page's.** The choice is a pair of signals in the list, not a route field. The entry route carries the entry, anchor, book and section and is built in many places; a filter that Back walks is not worth a new field on each. DESIGN.md's "filter state is route state" holds for the list pages and is not extended to this view, and the cost is that Back does not undo a choice and a shared link opens unfiltered. A later change can move the two choices into the route if reviewers miss it.
- **The old panel goes.** The class page's `Archetypes` disclosure (a list grouped by book) is not drawn when the list is in the page; the list shows the book on each row. The `Show 1 archetypes` plural was already fixed in the panel; the count line uses the same `pluralize`.
