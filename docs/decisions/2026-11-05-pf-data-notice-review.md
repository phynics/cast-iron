# Pf Data notice review, 2026-11-05

- **The owner accepted d20pfsrd.com's Section 15 export as a pinned notice source.** The CSV is committed verbatim at `sources/pathfinder-data-1-e/section15/d20pfsrd-section15.csv` with `d20pfsrd-section15.provenance.json`, which records the source, the owner's 2026-11-05 supply date, the SHA-256, and the 1,244-row count. `pf_legal` verifies the hash and count before matching and fails on any mismatch. d20pfsrd.com distributes its PF1e Open Game Content under the Open Game License 1.0a; this evidence covers notice text, not permission to import new content.
- **Notice sources are ordered and additive.** `pf_legal` matches each publication against the Pf Data `OPENGAMECONTENT.md` list, then PSRD's `legal-notices.json`, then the d20pfsrd export. Within a source the rules remain exact title, reviewed alias, then the deterministic normalizer. Every source is tried, so a publication that already matched keeps later distinct notices; the report records the first source as `match_method` (`exact`, `normalized-prefix`, or `alias` for Pf Data; `psrd` or `d20pfsrd` otherwise) and every contributor in `notice_sources`. Notice titles from the export are the text before the first `©` or `Copyright` marker with trailing sentence punctuation removed; notice text is trimmed only.
- **Six aliases were reviewed in this pass.** The earlier three are `Heroes of the Streets` → `Pathfinder Player Companion: Heroes of the Street`, `Pathfinder #119: Prisoners of the Blight` → `Pathfinder Adventure Path #119: Prisoner of the Blight`, and `Realm of the Fellnight Queen` → `Pathfinder Module: Realm of the Fellknight Queen`. The d20pfsrd review adds `Pathfinder Campaign Setting` → `Pathfinder Chronicles Campaign Setting`, `Revenge of the Kobold King` → `Pathfinder Module D1.5: Revenge of the Kobold King`, and `Tome of Horrors Complete (PF)` → `Tome of Horrors Complete`. No alias uses a similarity score. The export's two *Rival Guide* author lists and two 2008 Campaign Setting variants are both retained.
- **`Pathfinder Campaign Setting` is the 2008 book, not a generic label.** It is *Pathfinder Chronicles Campaign Setting* (Paizo product `v5748btpy84eo`). The registry's 84 references now have that book's notice; the label itself no longer blocks a record. Four records name only this label and are covered directly; the other 80 also name at least one other publication. The earlier note that four records could not be re-attributed is superseded: the alias supplies the attribution without changing their source markers.
- **The union restores most blocked records.** The legal report moves from 400 to 431 matched publications and from 53 to 22 unmatched. The canonical legal screen rejects 359 records, down from 1,226. The remaining exclusions are named in `reports/migration-coverage.json` and `reports/migration-test.json`, and the shipped legal page plus `SourceInfo.copyright` carry every matched notice verbatim. This changes legal data and the report shape only; `SCHEMA_VERSION` and `INDEX_FORMAT_VERSION` are unchanged.
- **Absent titles stay unmatched, not aliased by resemblance.** The 22 remaining labels are *Bestiary 6*, *Faiths of Purity*, *Goblins of Golarion*, *Guide to Darkmoon Vale*, *Horror Realms*, *Inner Sea Taverns*, *Kingmaker Player's Guide*, *Magic of Thassilon*, *Pathfinder #64*, *Serpent's Skull Player's Guide*, *We Be Goblins!*, and the 11 Dynamite comic issues. The eligible books need a complete Section 15 line from a primary source. The 17 d20pfsrd rows whose title cannot be separated from the copyright text (misspellings such as `Copynght`) appear under `unparsed_notices` rather than disappearing.
